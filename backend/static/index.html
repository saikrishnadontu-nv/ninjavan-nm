How the numbers are worked out

AC2 is the warehouse stock you can actually sell, in the same units as AMAST. Bundles and cartons are converted using the unit conversion list, locations and lots are added up per product, and damaged stock and stock inside its outbound life (near-expiry or expired) is taken off, as ITO does.

ITO is “Available to sell” plus “Reserved”. AMAST is the “Fresh” figure. The “after orders” column is AMAST’s own Available figure, which is Fresh minus orders AMAST has already taken.

Δ AC2 → ITO is ITO minus AC2. Δ ITO → AMAST is AMAST minus ITO. AMAST vs AC2 is the total gap. A positive total means AMAST shows more than the warehouse has, so orders may fail. A negative total means stock is being missed, so sales may be lost.

Clearance products (starting with “(CLR)”) and test products are left out. Every difference is flagged, however small.
